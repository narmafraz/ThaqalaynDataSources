"text": "Riwāyatun ‘an Abī al-‘Abbās Muḥammad ibn Ibrāhīm ibn Isḥāq al-Ṭālkānī (ḍiya’ Allāhu ‘anhu) qāla: Riwāyatun ‘an Abī Sa‘īd al-Ḥasan ibn ‘Alī al-‘Adawī, ‘an ‘Ibād ibn Suhayb, ‘an abīhi, ‘an jaddihi, ‘an al-Rabī‘ ṣāḥib al-Manṣūr, qāla: ḥaḍara Abū ‘Abdillāh Ja‘far ibn Muḥammad al-Ṣādiq (‘alayhi al-salām) majlis al-Manṣūr yawman, wa-‘indahu rajulun min al-Hind yaqra’ kutub al-ṭibb, faj‘ala Abū ‘Abdillāh al-Ṣādiq Ja‘far ibn Muḥammad (‘alayhimā al-salām) yanṣitu li-qirā’atihi, falammā faragha al-Hindī qāla lahu: Yā Abā ‘Abdillāh, aturīdu mimmā ma‘ayya shay’ān? Qāla: Lā, fa-inna mā ma‘ayya khayrun mimmā ma‘aka. Qāla: Wa-mā huwa? Qāla: Udāwi al-ḥār bi-l-bārid, wa-l-bārid bi-l-ḥār, wa-l-rutb bi-l-yābis, wa-l-yābis bi-l-rutb, wa-uridu al-amra kullahu ilā Allāh (‘azza wa-jalla), wa-astakhdimu mā qāla rasūluhi (ṣallā Allāhu ‘alayhi wa-ālihi): al-ma‘idatu baytu al-dā’ wa-l-ḥamiyyatu al-dawā’, wa-u‘āwidu al-badana mā i‘tāda. Qāla al-Hindī: Wa-hal al-ṭibb illā hādhā? Qāla al-Ṣādiq (‘alayhi al-salām): Afatarānī ‘an kutub al-ṭibb akhaththu? Qāla: Na‘am. Qāla: Lā wallāhi mā akhaththu illā ‘an All
