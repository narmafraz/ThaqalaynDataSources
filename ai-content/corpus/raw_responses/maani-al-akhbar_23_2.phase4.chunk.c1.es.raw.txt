"text": "Kunā ma'a Ibn 'Abbās bi-l-Ta'if, anā wa Abū al-Haliya wa Sa'īd ibn Jubayr wa 'Ikrima. Ja'a al-mu'adhdhin fa-qāla: Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar. Wa kāna ism al-mu'adhdhin Qatāma ibn 'Abd al-Raḥmān al-Thaqafī. Fa-qāla Ibn 'Abbās: A-ta'lamūna mā qāla al-mu'adhdhin? Fa-qāla lahu Abū al-Haliya: Akhbirnā bi-ta'wīlihi. Fa-qāla Ibn 'Abbās: Idhā qāla al-mu'adhdhin: Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, fa-innamā yaqūlu: Yā ayyuhā al-mushghalūn bi-l-ard, qad faradati-l-ṣalātu fa-inbi'tū ilayhā. Wa idhā qāla: Ashhadu an lā ilāha illā Allāh, fa-innamā yaqūlu: Yawma al-qiyāmati tashhadu lī mā fī al-samāwāti wa mā fī al-ard bi-annī aḥadathtum khamsa marrāt. Wa idhā qāla: Wa ashhadu anna Muḥammadan rasūlu Allāh, fa-innamā yaqūlu: Tanaṣṣalu al-qiyāma wa tashhadu lī Muḥammadun 'indakum bi-annī aḥadathtum bi-dhālika khamsa marrāt wa qad istaqamat hujjati 'alā Allāh. Wa idhā qāla: Ḥāsu ilā-l-ṣalāti, fa-innamā yaqūlu: Dīnun ḥaq, fa-aqīmūhu. Wa idhā qāla: Ḥāsu ilā-l-fawz, fa-innamā yaqūlu: A'tū ilā ta'āti Allāh wa khudhū naṣībakum min raḥmati Allāh, ya'ni al-jamā'a. Wa idhā qāla al-'abd: Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, fa-innamā yaqūlu: Qad hurimat al-a'māl (aw: rufi'a 'an
