"text": "Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn al-Hasan al-Saffar from Muhammad ibn Isa said: Khishf, mother of the concubine of Isa ibn Ali ibn Yaqtin, wrote to him in the year 230 AH, asking about the marriage of her daughter to al-Husayn ibn Ubayd. She said: \"I inform you, O my master and lord, that the daughter of your master, Isa ibn Ali ibn Yaqtin, I acquired from the son of Ubayd ibn Yaqtin. After I acquired her, they mentioned that her grandmother, Umm Isa ibn Ali ibn Yaqtin, belonged to Ubayd ibn Yaqtin, then she went to Ali ibn Yaqtin, and he fathered Isa from her. They mentioned that the son of Ubayd has become her uncle from the side of her grandmother, the mother of her father, because she belonged to Ubayd ibn Yaqtin. So your opinion, O my master and lord, is to bestow upon your mistress an explanation from you and inform me if it is permissible for him, for your mistress, O my master, is in grief, of which Allah is aware.\" He wrote upon it between the lines: \"When he becomes an uncle, it is not permissible for him, and the uncle is like a father.\" Muhammad ibn al-Hasan said: The author of this book, this hadith is like the hadith of Zayd ibn al-Jahm and al-Husayn ibn Khalid al-Sayrafī regarding the case where a man has a concubine, he has intercourse with her, then she goes to another man, and is blessed with children from the second man; it is not permissible to marry her children to his children from the other master, due to his having had intercourse with her. And we have clarified that this is based on a type of dislike, and there is no difference whether the child was born before or after the intercourse, that this is not prohibited. And this report may imply that he became her uncle because his grandmother, when she belonged to Ubayd ibn Yaqtin, gave birth to him, al-Husayn ibn Ubayd ibn Yaqtin, and the report does not state that al-Husayn was not from her. Then when she was brought to Ali ibn Yaqtin, she also gave birth to Isa from him, so they became brothers from the mother's side and cousins from the father's side. So if Isa was blessed with a daughter, this al-Husayn ibn Ubayd from her mother's side becomes her uncle. And if al-Husayn ibn Ubayd had been born from another woman, the daughter of Isa would not be prohibited
