"text": "El Tawq: Alá al-A'la al-Yatimmu umrak wa yu'arrifuka kulla khayr, wa yakhim bihi a'malaka. Nabbigha man tuthbitu lahu al-din wa tathiqqu bihi al-niyya min ikhwana alina — yu'ayyidukal lah — anna Muhammad ibn Ali, al-mashhur bi al-Shalmaghani — zhad Ibn Dawud: wa huwa min alladhina asra'allah bihi al-sakht wa lam yu'tih al-nu'ah — qad radda 'an al-Islam wa kharaja minhu — ijma' — wa ahdatha bidin al-lah bid'a, wa zadda fihi ma kafara bihi al-khaliq — Harun: fi ma zadda — al-jalil al-muta'ali. Wa zadda kadhban wa dhalalan, wa qala dhanban kabira — Harun: amran kabira — al-muqadduna bi al-lah kadhabu wa dalu wa khaba. Wa kunnah al-lah al-'ali wa rasuluhu wa ahlihi, sallal-lah 'alayhim wa sallam wa rahmatuh wa barakatuh, minhu, wa la'nah bi la'ni al-lah — ijma' — [Ibn Dawud zhad: fi al-tabi'a] min al-zahir minna wa al-batin, fi al-sirr wa al-'ani, fi kulli waqt, wa fi kulli hal, wa 'ala kulli man shadda bihi aw taba'a aw wasalahu hadhihi al-qawla minna wa thabata 'ala hujjatih minhu. Nabbighuhum — al-Saymari: yakhfakal lah — [Ibn Dhaka: yu'ayyidakal lah] annahum 'ala hifz — Ibn Dawud: i'lam annahum 'ala hifz minhu — [Harun: wa nabbighuhum annahum 'ala hifz] wa hitha' minhu — Ibn Dawud wa Harun: bi tariqati aslafina fi azh-hamihi — al-Saymari: bi tariqati kunnah fi azh-hamihi min azh-hamihi — Ibn Dhaka: bi tariqati ahaduna man qaddama fi azh-hamihi — [ijma'] kama al-Shari'i, al-Numayri, al-Hilali, al-Bilali, wa ghayruhum.
