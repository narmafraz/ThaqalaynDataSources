"text": "Muhammad ibn Musa al-Mutawakkil narrated, saying: Ali ibn Ibrahim narrated from Abdullah ibn Ahmad al-Mosuli, from Saqr ibn Abi Dalaf, who said: When al-Mutawakkil carried our Master, Abu al-Hasan (peace be upon him), I came to ask about his news. He said: Then al-Zarraq looked at me, and he was a chamberlain for al-Mutawakkil, and he gestured to me to enter, so I entered. He said: O Saqr, what is your matter? I said: Good, O teacher. He said: Sit. Then I was taken by what preceded and what followed, and I said: I erred in coming. He said: He then dismissed the people from him, and said: What is your matter? And for what did you come? I said: For some news. He said: Perhaps you came to ask about the news of your Master? I said to him: And who is my Master? My Master is the Commander of the Believers. He said: Be silent, your Master is the Truth, so do not be shy of me, for I am on your sect. I said: Praise be to Allah. He said: Do you wish to see him? I said: Yes. He said: Sit until the courier leaves from him. He said: So I sat, and when he left, he said to a young man of his: Take Saqr by the hand and enter him into the chamber in which is the imprisoned Alawi, and leave between him and him. He said: So he entered me into the chamber and gestured to a room, so I entered. He said: And there he was, peace be upon him, sitting on the middle of a straw mat, and opposite him was a dug grave. He said: So I offered peace, and he replied, then commanded me to sit. Then he said to me: O Saqr, what brought you? I said: My Master, I came to know your news. He said: Then I looked at the grave and wept. He looked at me and said: O Saqr, let it not be upon you, they will not reach us with harm. I said: Praise be to Allah. Then I said: O my Master, a narration was reported from the Prophet (peace be upon him and his family) whose meaning I do not know. He said: And what is it? I said: His saying: 'Do not antagonize the Days, lest they antagonize you.' What is its meaning? He said: Yes, the Days are us, as long as the heavens and the earth are established.
